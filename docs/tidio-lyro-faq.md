## Q&A knowledge (paste each into Lyro → Knowledge → Q&A)

### Shipping & delivery

**Q: Do you offer free shipping?**
A: Yes — free shipping on all items to the continental U.S. (Free Budget Shipping, about 8–14 business days transit). Faster options like USPS Priority (3–7 business days) are available at checkout. Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories ship expedited with additional charges. We ship from Sylmar, California, and larger orders may arrive in multiple shipments.

**Q: How fast will my order ship?**
A: Orders ship within 1–2 business days after payment is confirmed (processing is Monday–Friday). Then it's transit time with the carrier: Free Budget Shipping is 8–14 business days, USPS Priority is 3–7 business days.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
A: Not at this time — we ship only within the U.S. (the 48 continental states, plus Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories). Non-U.S. buyers can contact our Business Development team about partner-channel options at admin@platinummicro.com.

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: You'll get tracking by email once your order ships, and you can track directly with the carrier (UPS or USPS). You can also see status under Order History after signing in.

### Returns & warranty

**Q: What is your return policy?**
A: We have a 30-day return/exchange policy from the purchase date. All items ship brand new with the manufacturer warranty. To start a return or RMA, email admin@platinummicro.com or ask here and we'll walk you through it.

**Q: Are there items that can't be returned?**
A: Yes. Non-returnable items are open software, digital downloads, software keycards, online subscriptions, and any products from LG, GIGABYTE, and FORTINET. Everything else falls under the standard 30-day return/exchange policy.

**Q: Do your products come with a warranty?**
A: Yes — every item ships brand new with the full manufacturer warranty. Bulk and OEM products may come without retail packaging or accessories, but they still carry the full manufacturer warranty. We're not responsible for physical damage, misuse, or anything that voids the manufacturer warranty.

**Q: Is your hardware new or refurbished?**
A: Both. Most of our catalog is brand new with manufacturer warranty; we also carry a certified-refurbished line (tested and warrantied, up to 60% off list — use code REFURB60). Each product page notes its condition.

### Pricing, quotes & programs

**Q: Can I get a volume or bulk price?**
A: Absolutely. Share your SKUs and quantities here (or use Quick Order on the site) and our team turns around a written volume quote, usually within one business day. We price honestly and tell you real lead times up front.

**Q: How does "Request a Quote" work?**
A: Many items — especially servers and configured builds — are priced by quote. Add what you need and hit Request a Quote, or just tell me the SKUs and quantities here, and a specialist sends written pricing back, usually within one business day.

**Q: Do you work with cooperative or government purchasing contracts?**
A: Yes. You can buy through OMNIA Partners (our lead cooperative vehicle), TD SYNNEX authorized distribution, and GSA-aligned pricing for federal and DoD buyers (on request). We're also SBA Small Business certified and eligible for small-business set-aside contracts.

**Q: Do you offer net terms / trade credit?**
A: Yes — Net-30 trade credit is available on approved application. Ask us here and we'll send you the application.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Major payment methods at checkout, plus Net-30 trade credit on approved application. For purchase orders, ACH, or wire, contact us at admin@platinummicro.com and we'll set it up.

**Q: I'm tax-exempt — how do I set that up?**
A: Send your tax-exempt certification to tax-exempt@platinummicro.com and we'll apply it to your account and orders.

**Q: Are there any current discount codes?**
A: Yes — use SAVE10 for 10% off all HPE ProLiant servers, and REFURB60 on our certified-refurbished enterprise gear (up to 60% off list).

### Who we are

**Q: Who do you sell to?**
A: For two decades we've distributed enterprise IT from Southern California to system integrators, public-sector buyers, K–12 and higher education, healthcare networks, and state, local, and federal government — with 40+ manufacturer partnerships.

**Q: Where are you located and where do you ship from?**
A: We're based in Sylmar, California (15815 Monte Street, Suite 103, Sylmar, CA 91342) and ship from there.

It usually takes under one business day. First-time buyers may be asked to confirm payment method; high-value orders may need a signed quote acknowledgement.

### Products, brands & catalog

**Q: What product categories do you carry?**
A: Servers, Storage, Components, Networking, Computers, Peripherals, Software, and pre-configured Bundles. Tell me what you're after and I'll point you straight to it.

**Q: What brands do you carry?**
A: We're a direct authorized reseller for 21 manufacturers and partner with 26 more — HPE, Intel, AMD, Samsung, Seagate, Western Digital, and many others. Every unit carries the original manufacturer warranty. Don't see your brand? Tell me the manufacturer and rough quantity and our team can usually source it.

**Q: Do you carry HPE ProLiant servers?**
A: Yes — HPE ProLiant is one of our core server lines, and right now SAVE10 takes 10% off all HPE ProLiant servers. Tell me the model or your workload (compute, storage, GPU, density) and I'll help you find the right fit or get a custom build quoted.

**Q: Do you have a specific product / is it in stock / what's the price?**
A: Tell me the product name, model, or SKU and I'll pull it up with live pricing and stock. You can also search the site or browse from the main menu.

### Account & tools

**Q: How do I create an account or sign in?**
A: Click "Sign in" in the header. We use Shopify's secure sign-in — enter your email and you'll get a one-time login link. Once in, you can see orders, addresses, and your saved lists.

**Q: Can I save products to compare or order later?**
A: Yes — sign in and use Lists to save products to named lists (like a server build) and return to them anytime.

**Q: How do I place a large order quickly?**
A: Use Quick Order to add multiple SKUs with quantities at once. For tiered or volume pricing, send the list here or Request a Quote and we'll respond, usually within one business day.

**Q: How do I reach a human / what are your hours?**
A: Call (877) PMG-4YOU, Monday–Friday 7:00am–5:00pm Pacific — calls go to a real person. Or email admin@platinummicro.com (checked all day), or leave your name and email here and a specialist will follow up.

### Responsibility

**Q: What do you do with e-waste / recycling?**
A: We comply with California's Electronic Waste Recycling Act and collect state e-waste fees on covered display devices, routing them to certified recyclers. If you have end-of-life equipment, ask us and we can recommend a state-licensed e-waste handler.
